Send your files

Email your files to tyrellmatekwa@gmail.com.

  • Stems as 24-bit WAV, 44.1 to 48 kHz.
  • Name every track clearly, for example Lead Vox, Kick, Bass.
  • Export from the start of the session so no clips are missing.
  • Send reference tracks at least 24 hours before your session.
  • Files too big for email? Send a download link instead.
